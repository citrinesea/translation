Chapter 1

A few days later.

Phew... Today’s practice went off without a hitch as well.

Yeah. Succession Match just wrapped up too, so AKATSUKI’s gettin’ to a good place slowly but surely.

… Still. Ya good, Danna? Ya seem strangely tired.

Me? I didn’t really mean to come off that way, but...

With Succession Match and Unification by Force having just happened, it’s been pretty hectic lately. I might be a little burnt out.

Burnt out, huh…?

Ahh. Guess that’s what you were talkin’ about when you said you were feelin’ unwell before?

Jobs have been hard to come by ever since we got to Rhythm Link, so we've been givin’ it our all for the ones we do get.

That's right. I can't say I know know the cause for sure, but that’s what I suspect.

I chased a longtime, dearest wish with Sakuma in Succession Match, and clawed and struggled to be recognized by the Rhythm Link veterans in Unification by Force.

We'd spent so much time just sitting around, sprinting forward so suddenly might've been what exhausted me.

Haha. Now that ya say that, we have had our hands full with huge jobs back-to-back.

Especially you, Danna. As the leader, you worked at it harder than any of us.

You're probably used to organizin' projects, but doin' somethin' you're not used to like a live with tons of action scenes... Figures you'd be totally beat from it all.

Don’t push yourself, Hasumi-dono. What will happen to AKATSUKI if you collapse?

You're worrying too much. I'm mindful of my health more than most. I had a childhood friend who’d get sick easily, after all.

That aside, practice is over. Let’s pick a spot to settle down and start the brieifing.

Like I mentioned before, I wanted to talk to you guys about AKATSUKI's plans for Halloween.

Yeah. I wanted to ask about that too.

It’s been goin’ around that AKATSUKI’s not gonna participate in the ES Halloween event. Is that actually true?

When I ran into Nito at the office, he said somethin’ like “If AKATSUKI’s going to do the agency’s live, leave ES Halloween to me.”

…? What…? I said I was still considering it, though.

Well, that’s a problem. Plenty of idols come in and out of the office. Someone must have seen me listening to what a senior had to say and got the wrong idea.

But what about the Rhythm Link joint live? I'm no expert, but is it that good of a live that we're prioritizing that over ES Halloween?

My kid sister looks forward to Halloween every year. I figure Halloween’s what's all the rage for the young crowd, right?

Of course, your perception of Halloween is right, Kiryu.

Still, I see the benefits of forgoing Halloween just this once vastly outweigh the ones we get if we participate.

Halloween, as it were, is a relatively new event with a short history in Japan. It’s only natural the middle-aged seniors of Rhythm Link find it unfamiliar.

Coming at it a different way, rumor has it that this is our chance to butter them up.

I’ve had my concerns about how the higher-ups operate too, but if you want to get close enough to strike while they sleep, you need to curry their favor to get close enough in the first place.

… I suppose that's coming from years of dealing with politics in the Student Council. But if you want to participate in ES Halloween, Kiryu, I can withdraw us from Rhythm Festa right away.

No. You’ve saved my skin time ‘n time again. Whatever you decide, I’ll follow your lead.

I feel the exact same as Kiryu-dono. Hasumi-dono is the head of AKATSUKI, so we’ve left it to you to decide the course we take.

I understand. Well, here’s what I’m thinking right now.

Rhythm Link has many, many untapped resources— there's value in the ones people have stopped paying attention to.

That's what happened with Vampire Shogun, after all. Rhythm Link holds the rights to plenty of works that used to be popular.

AKATSUKI might get great benefit out of making use of those works' name recognition. Covers, revivals, that sort of thing.

All that to say, I went ahead and pulled together all the works that Rhythm Link holds the rights to.

Ohh. I was wondering what was in that paper bag. This is quite the large collection.

Not bad. Picking between Rhythm Link and Halloween's gettin’ real interestin’ all of a sudden ♪

No point standing around and talking about it here. Let’s go grab some grub while we talk it over.

Right. We’ve been treated like newbies up to this point, but now we’re finally being seen as "young go-getters". It’s about time we made an even bigger splash.

Tradition and innovation— that is AKATSUKI's strategy. We'll take this opportunity and make some broad steps towards expanding our influence within the agency.

Chapter 2

Scores of minutes later. Cafe COCHI.

No good. I don't get it at all.

What's this "Frankenmagistrate" thing? There's so many of these works I dunno, lookin' 'em up one-by-one on my phone's gettin' annoying.

Ah, right. It was a property trying to cash in on the success of Vampire Shogun

It’s a courtroom setting where Frankenmagistrate, a well-renowned judge, though not to the point where he’s timelessly famous, pulls back the curtain and reveals the true culprits in each trial.

There weren’t any reruns, so I’ve only heard of it, though. It didn't get a sequel like Vampire Shogun did either.

Hmm. So… with a judge-character as the lead role, it is a work similar to a detective-story.

Kiryu-dono. Could you look up more information on the “in-taa-netto” for me, so we can understand what kind of show this is?

Gimme a sec. I'm pullin’ it up.

But I can't fact check as thoroughly as you, Hasumi-no-danna, so, well, just be patient.

Sorry, Kiryu. Thanks for going to the trouble of taking on the research for me.

No big deal. You’re always doin’ everything for us, like makin’ this list.

I’m good with tinkerin’ on machines, but high-tech stuff? Not so much.

It seems like all kindsa media like online videos have been replacin’ TV these days. I feel like I hafta start gettin’ used to all these newfangled things.

Heheh. I’m sure you’ll pick it up quickly with how skillful you are, Kiryu.

Besides, there are old works who don’t have any information about them on the internet. It’s not a matter of whether you’re good at it or not. It’d end up taking a long time, even for me.

Oh. Here it is.

Looks like there’s some kinda Frankenmagistrate fansite. Seems to be someone’s personal site, and it looks pretty old, but it’s got full records of news about the show and their personal thoughts about it.

Hmm… It details that the difference between Frankenmagistrate and Vampire Shogun is that Frankenmagistrate was a half-baked blend between a period drama and a monster story, and as a result, it did not stand the tests of time.

Frankenmagistrate was not a traditional period drama that based itself in the concept of good and evil, but in the worries that come with being a living creature, and that is what had mixed reception.

Hmm. Guess there are reasons some masterpieces stick around and reasons others don’t, huh!

“It was an almost unexpected endeavor in blending science fiction with historical period drama…”? Just readin’ this makes me wanna watch it, kinda.

Perhaps that is exactly what makes devoted fans such as this keep such detailed records.

...

Hey, what’s up? You’ve been silent, y’know?

Haha, could it be that yer interested in Frankenmagistrate? ♪

Ahh, no... Well, I'd be lying if I said I wasn't interested.

But popularity is important when you're picking a cover song. Even if you manage to get the super-fans to listen to it, it’s a gamble whether or not they’ll actually take to it.

Yeah, guess so. Welp, guess this Frankenmagistrate thing’s a bust.

… Yeah, you’re right.

Hm? You seem strangely evasive, Hasumi-dono. Is something the matter?

Not really. I just feel a little sorry for Frankenmagistrate with the reception it received from the public.

The way they aimed to be something so cutting-edge, yet came across as half-baked— I can tell they did what they wanted to achieve, but as an artistic person, it’s conflicting to me.

Hmm… The public certainly may prefer the tried-and-true path.

We AKATSUKI are “aidoru” who base ourselves in tradition as well. It seems we need to keep in mind not to make the same mistakes as we balance the new with the old, hm?

You’re real serious Kanzaki. You don’t gotta take everything so seriously, y’know?

Maybe Frankenmagistrate was just too ahead of its time. I mean, nowadays, Japan’s pretty eager takin’ on stuff from the West, right?

Certainly, “Ha-rou-iin” is such an event.

Western culture… What was once undesirable in one generation has become quite popular with the current generation.

Perhaps “Harou-in” has become a substitute for hometown festivals for those who live in the city.

The experience of such a day is rare in this day and age. Our unit lives may very well be descended from an unbroken lineage of festivities since time immemorial too.

Maybe. Now, that’s enough of Frankenmagistrate. We should take a look at the next work.

There are plenty of works to uncover beneath the passage of time. At this pace, we’ll be here all night.

Yeah, yer right. Welp, the next one we gotta look up is, “The Family-Man Yakuza.”

Understood. Haha, this work seems perfectly suited for you, Kiryu-dono.
