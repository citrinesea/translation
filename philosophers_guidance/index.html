Translation Notes

  1. ↑ This happens in Flower Festival.
  2. ↑ 栄養ドリンク (eiyou dorinku) have both nutritional qualities and energy qualities, and are used for fatigue recovery and nutritional supplementation. These are different from エナジードリンク (enajii dorinku), which are more like our sugary American energy drinks.
  3. ↑ This might be about the secret mission Keito gives Mao in Agents.
  4. ↑ This is about $1500 USD.
  5. ↑ Foie gras is a luxury French delicacy made from duck or goose. Do note that its production is considered unethical for animal welfare.
  6. ↑ Meteorology note: Technically the eye of the hurricane is the calmest part of the storm. He probably means the eyewall, the most turbulent part.
  7. ↑ ほうじ茶 (houjicha) is a green tea that's roasted at high temperatures instead of being left unroasted.
  8. ↑ Starlight Festival is an S1 Dream Festival themed around Christmas that occurs in Starlight Festival.
  9. ↑ SS is a showdown between Trickstar and Eden that occurs in this story.
  10. ↑ Eichi establishes the new S3 rank of Dream Festivals starting with the story Dream Travel.
  11. ↑ Starry Night Festival is a B1, featuring a large unnamed temporary unit including Nazuna and Shinobu, that takes place in the story Starry Night Festival.
