(“The theatre troupe Dramatica is having its next performance as an original musical. The script-writer is AKATSUKI’s Hasumi Keito, so you don’t want to miss it ☆”)

(And yet we haven’t decided what the play is going to contain at all.)

(This certainly isn’t my first time creating something original, so it should be fine, and I intended to take it on, but…)

(You have to approach stageplay scripts very differently from manga, so it’ll be quite hard.)

(Still, I can’t run away now. It’s already been advertised. No, he made it so I couldn’t back out, didn’t he?)

(Anyways, I’ve been thinking it over but absolutely nothing is coming to mind. I’ll try and get a rough draft going.)

Oh! There you are! Keito, ucchuuu~ ☆

… What the… I wanted to be by myself, what are you doing here?

Mnn, what reason could there be for wanting to be alone?

Ohh, hold on! You don’t need to answer that! Lemme think, I’m brainstorming.

I get it now, so you had your heart broken, huh! Your lectures may be long, but your face is as pretty as can be, Keito.

That must mean you either have a dreadful taste in people, or your glasses fogged up and you asked out some weirdo by mistake!

I feel like I could compose a song based off that alone, wahahaha ☆

Cut it out. I’m not heartbroken or anything.

(Sigh) … If you don’t have work to do, go home, you’re being a hindrance. I’m busy writing the script for Dramatica. I don’t have the time to deal with you.

Hey, I have work! I’m in charge of the music that needs to go with that script of yours, after all! Hey, why don’t we talk it through together?

Show me, show me, I’ll write you a symphony in an instant!

Hold on, I’m still in the brainstorming stages. It’s hard to come up with something completely original.

I was thinking it could be set in a school based off of the masterpiece Le Miserablés…

Huh~? Based off of Le Miserables? That story is about the life of a man earning 19 years of jail time just for stealing a loaf of bread!

It’s just based off the story. Didn’t I say it took place in a school?

Uuu~ Yuck! I hate it! The fact it’s such a dark, miserable, gloomy story is bad enough, it’s gotta be way brighter! Let’s make it a pleasant, happy story that leaves everyone smiling!

— Ah, hold on! I’ve just been struck by inspiration!

Why don’t you make it a story about aliens? I feel like Le Miserables definitely gives “alien mothership”!

… What?

Because of the aliens! The aliens!

Our story begins when the spaceship “Le Miserables” crashes into the earth. A story where the Miserablé Aliens make friends with the high schoolers of Earth…☆

Hey, hold on a second, don’t go firing yourself up.

The theme song should go like this, right? (Humming) ♪♪♪

I can’t say I'd have thought of that. Don’t just start on the composition as you please. It’s annoying that it’s actually starting to sound good…

(But… A story about aliens making friends with high school students, huh?)

(When it comes to the acting, Hibiki and Ran could be the aliens. Both of them have that otherworldly atmosphere to them. That should work.)

(As for the role of the high school students… how about Mashiro and Hidaka? If the aliens are the main attraction, then the side element, the humans, should be made up of ordinary people.)

(Those two would be perfect for that, then.)

(Mnn… The casting makes itself. It’s frustrating having to go along with Tsukinaga’s whims, but…)

(If it features high school students, it could be the “high school story” I wanted to make to begin with.)

(It ended up being a complete pivot from “Le Miserables” , but… it certainly meets the goal of “putting on an original musical”.)

(You can’t just stick to the first idea you come up with. There might be the possibility it takes you nowhere.)

(A play is something that all the members of the troupe create together after all. This time, I’ll go with Tsukinaga’s lead.)

(It’s a shame I wasn’t able to make it all by myself, but that’s a skill issue on my part. I’ll just have to use this as a learning experience for next time.)

Hmmn hmn~n ♪ Here’s where the aliens make their entrance, ta daaah! They beam down from the heavens like rain…☆

Ah… Hey, Tsukinaga! Don’t just grab my notebook and use it like it’s yours!

Ugyaa!? Give it back, give iiiiiit—! You’re kidnapping my composition baby~!

Don’t use other people’s things without permission. Carry your own notebook with you!

Alright, I get it already! I’ll just write on the table then!

You’re not a student anymore, so stop writing on others’ property! Keep that liberty in your own head!

Honestly… It can’t be helped. If you have to have something to write on *right now, *you can use the back of these documents.

Thanks! I love you, Keito~☆

Don’t say something so repulsive.

… Tsukinaga. After further consideration, I’ve decided to take your ideas into account when I’m working on the script.

I never would have thought of making an alien the main character. Thank you.

Wahahaha ☆ You don’t have to thank me, just hurry up and write the script! I’m itching, I can’t wait to see it!

Can you get it done in the next few hours? I can wait here.

Hold on, it’s going to take me a few days to get this script written. You’ll be waiting here a long while.

Hu~hhh? I’m not going to wait that long, you know!

I know! Alrighty, I’m going to start writing “The Song that Roots Keito On!” So finish it fast ☆

Don’t say something so absurd. I can’t come up with ideas out of thin air nearly as much as you can no matter how hard I try.

Just sit still for even just a minute. Isn’t there a way you can turn off that gushing faucet of inspiration of yours?

Honestly, I envy you to an extent, but you’re a truly incorrigible guy, aren’t you?
