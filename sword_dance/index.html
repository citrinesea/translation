En-- garde...![^]

998! 999!

One... thousaaand!

Mm. That might be enough practice swinging for today.

Recently, I have thrown myself entirely into activities with AKATSUKI and the Marine Life Club, so I have not had the proper time to delve into my training.

Both my sword and my aptitude might rust away.

If I neglect my training, I cannot progress. Martial arts are a method of mental cultivation, and so they should prove useful for life as an idol as well.)

(I shall face the battleground called the idol industry with a forged mind and body.)

(Besides, I cannot be at ease without my daily training. The time I spend swinging my blade with complete presence of thought is key to maintaining an inner peace in my life.)

(Still. This blade is too good at slicing. It is so good at slicing through the air that it gains too much force.)

(If I swing it too much, I may strain my glutes and ankles. Getting hurt would hinder my “aidoru” activities.)

(I wonder if I should prepare something like a wooden sword for my training instead…?)

(Nay. If I brace myself and concentrate, I shall not be defeated. Swinging a sheathed blade creates a sense of tension. If I train aimlessly I might as well not train at all.)

(Mhm. Well then, I shall begin my practice swings again!)

(Three thousand in the morning, seven thousand in the evening— this is the Jigen-ryu[^] way where a second strike is unnecessary. En garde!)

… Mngh!?

Whoa, Anzu-dono. Why did you just throw an empty can at me?

It was sliced in half the second it hit my blade… Slicing through metal can chip the blade, so I’d really prefer not to do so, though. Did you have business with me?

Hmm. So you’re cleaning up trash around the school, hm, Anzu-dono?

Of course. After outsiders come from outside campus for our Dre Fes, they’re quite rude guests, so they leave trash like empty cans and snack bags lying all over the campus.

So it’s a part time job where you go around school grounds picking up the litter and disposing of it…? Thank you very much for your hard work, Anzu-dono ♪

Hmm. You were already going to the trouble of gathering together trash and dead leaves, and the wind draft from my practice swings were a problem?

I apologize, I didn’t notice it was trouble. But if that’s the case, you could have called out to me. Don’t do something so tomboyish as throwing an empty can at me out of nowhere.

I thought an assassin had come to attack me. Hmm, you called out to me but I didn’t notice… You say?

I suppose having too much concentration has its merits and weaknesses. I bow my head in shame once more for my discourtesy.

I know. In recompense for my inconvenience, I shall assist you in your school grounds clean up.

Training does not start and end with swinging my blade. The honest work of picking up trash is perfect for bolstering the mind and soul.

Anzu-dono is my “kurasu-ma-to,” and so any trouble of yours is a trouble of mine ♪

Don’t be so reserved. The grounds of Yumenosaki Academy are vast. If it is just you, twelve whole moons/a whole year would pass before you were finished cleaning it all.

Please rely on me. I have a great “ai-dee-a.”

If the wind from the swing of my sword blows the trash away, and we use that…

It is possible to gather all of the trash in the academy in one place by targeting the swing in a certain direction!

Please allow me! Kanzaki Souma will throw his entire being into this task! This is my Clean-Up-The-School Sword Dance!

Wait, please do not use my sword in such a manner! My blade is no skewer— Ah, and please do not put it in the fire!

Understood! Trust in this Kanzaki Souma, and we shall go onward ♪

Hahh! Take that! And that!

… How dangerous—! You threw another empty can, Anzu-dono!

If anything, perhaps catching me off guard with empty cans does count as training...? Ah, as expected of you, Purodyuusaa-dono!

Hm? “You’re the dangerous one,” you say? Certainly, if I were to carelessly strike Anzu-dono with my blade, it would be a great travesty…

I shall reflect on it. I should pick up the trash normally, I suppose.

I had thought it would be a good idea— being able to help Anzu-dono while training with my blade, but… It wasn’t so much “hitting two birds with one stone,” as much as it was “trying to do both and accomplishing neither.”

Anzu-dono, do you mean to say that you’ll show me how…?

How impressive! Splendid, splendid ♪

Hm? Lend you my blade, you say? That is dangerous, you do not know how to hold it.

If I were to let Anzu-dono get hurt, I would never be able to show my face to my brethren of Yumenosaki Academy ever again.

“Says you.” Well, you’re right, but… Hm? Are you making an open-air bonfire? You are burning the dried leaves you have gathered… Are you going to make sweet potatoes with them?

You’ve pierced a sweet potato with my sword to roast it over the open flame…

It smells rather delicious… All my training has made me a bit hungry too.

It’s covered in soot! I won’t be able to show my face to my ancestors now!

Uuu. Anzu-dono, you’re very harsh sometimes. Well, yes… A Japanese sword is rather useless in a school for Japanese idols.

If Producer-dono, someone who works hard and does so much for us day in and day out, needs a little assistance satisfying her appetite…

Then just like my blade, please use me to your heart’s content. First, shall we pick up the trash, Producer-dono?
