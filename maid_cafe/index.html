Translaton Notes

  1. ↑ At maid cafes, the maids ask the customer to do a "magical spell" with them to make the food or drink more delicious; you make heart hands, point them at the food lke a beam, "moe moe kyuuun" (萌え萌えキュン); this is what the idols do when you take the polaroids. Some cafes add phrases like "oishikuna~re" (become delicious), which is what Shinobu uses here.
