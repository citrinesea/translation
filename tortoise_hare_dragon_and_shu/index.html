2nd year of ES’s establishment, August, inside the helicopter.

To think I’d be on the same helicopter as you, Kiryu.

Yeah, it’s not too often we head home together, huh? Hrrp…

Your motion sickness is as terrible as ever.

You’ve been so composed on the MEGASPHERE, I’d figured you’d overcome your motion sickness for good.

It’s a talent of yours that you’re able to get so sick in the brief span of time it takes to get from the ground to the MEGASPHERE.

… Over… come it? Hasumi-no-danna's said something like that to me before…1

I keep from goin’ near the rim of the MEGASPHERE so I don’t look over the edge.

And every night, before I hit the hay, I sit in bed and just go, “You’re on the ground…. You’re on the ground…” ‘til I fall asleep, and end up forgetting all about it.

Since it’s huge, it’s like a floatin’ island, one big extension comin’ outta the ground. And that’s not a vehicle, now is it?

… Is that some kind of hypnosis you’re inducing on yourself?

Anyways. It seems that technique of yours isn’t as effective when you’re on a helicoptor approaching the MEGASPHERE itself.

I have an extra water bottle. Take it.

You’re a lifesaver… (Gulping it down)

Hahhh… I’m feelin’ a lil’ better now. Thanks, Itsuki.

What’s there to thank me for? I just wouldn’t be able to stand it if you threw up.

I’m not actually gonna hurl y’know. I’m gonna get through it one way or another.

Tch, I just thought about it again… Hrp…

… Sorry. Can we just talk about anythin’ else? Talkin’ with you keeps my mind off of it, but the second we start talkin’ about vehicles it makes me wanna throw up.

(Sigh) Honestly, you’re such a troublesome man… You’re big and bulky, and yet your disposition is like that of a maiden’s when it comes to vehicles.

Points of conversation then… Ever since we entered the MEGASPHERE as of late, we idols have been livestreaming. You AKATSUKI have been conducting fluff-streams as well, hm?

What’s that supposed to mean? Are you dissin’ us?

No. AKATSUKI has always been a unit that reinterprets Japanese culture for the modern age, so it makes sense well-enough.

I have no intention of disavowing the act of streaming itself. The cheapness of how every single idol on the ship has flocked to it is what disgusts me.

And it all stems from that wretched Nice… God shall strike him down any day now.

Icarus’s wings melted when he flew too close to the sun, and the Tower of Babel, which aimed to stretch into the sky towards God, was struck down. There are many cautionary tales for those humans who don’t know their place.

…

Are you listening to me, Kiryu?

Yeah, yeah I heardja.

It’s just… it’s makin’ me nostalgic, the way you go on about stuff no one gets. You’ve always been like that.

The things I’m saying aren’t confusing, you’re just slow to understand it.

… Haha. Well, it's not like it matters either way.

You used to go on ‘n on about how Aesop’s fables were way more refined than Japanese fairytales. You were a real riot back then.

Hmm? What are you talking about?

You don’t remember? Y’know, the story of the tortoise and the hare?

The tortoise and the hare…?

Yeah. I remember you said somethin’ like… Life is short, so even if the rabbit takes a nap, he’s the superior one ‘cuz he can get places faster.

So we actually tried to make a rabbit race a turtle.

I didn’t believe you, so I said I’d show you and went and grabbed a turtle from the river nearby.

We needed a rabbit too, but there wasn't one around, so I took its place and went and crawled on all-fours.

Hmph, I remember now. What a bunch of childish horseplay that was…

And I seem to recall some big foolish rabbit who ended up dozing off midway through just like in the story.

I was lettin’ him win, I was lettin’ him win. … And the turtle went in the opposite direction of the finish line, so we never ended up with a winner.

You remember the most pointless things. You ought to put your mind to better use.

Sorry, but I left my brain back in my Ma’s womb.

And in return she birthed me big and strong, so I’d say that’s all I need.

Here you are with your bravado. If you’re so big and strong, how do you explain your current predicament?

Well, when I think of your mother, I suppose I can understand that alone is enough.

… Ahem (Clearing his throat). Well, we should be arriving at the helipad any second now. You'll be fine from here, right?

Your motion-sickness did not worsen, and I was kept from being any more displeased. You can do what you like after.

Sure, but, why don’t we go back to the dorms together, Itsuki?

You’re just headin’ back too, right? We might as well go together, what’s the reason not to?

… Honestly… I don’t make it a hobby to look after the sickly, but...

I suppose I’ll have to keep you company. You did carry me on your back when I was sickly, after all.

Still. You really saved my skin back there.

Motion sickness always knocks me on my ass in that helicopter. Dunno what I’d’ve done without ya.

Well, it’s not like our conversation was as deep as solvin’ the world’s problems up there, but...

Maybe it’s ‘cuz we’ve got all that history as childhood buddies that I was able to forget the ride up and get lost in conversation.

… Oh yeah. Didja wanna go to the Passage Area? Figure I can pay you back for the water bottle you gave me. I’ll buy ya whatever drink your heart desires.

Hmph. I said there’s no need to thank me.

It’s not like you to be so thankful, it’s unsettling. Did the helicopter ride really make you feel that sick?

But… I see. If you insist.

It’s not like I won’t grace you with some conversation on the off chances we meet. Everyone needs some refined conversation now and again, hm?

Oh, shut it, Icchan. Who are you callin’ “unrefined”, huh?

Let’s have more casual chats, just like childhood friends would.

We were on the helicopter for that short a time, there wasn’t enough time for us to really catch up.

The next time we meet? Let’s go on ’n on about nonsense just like when we were brats ♪
