Heh. That sounds pretty easy. I should try it out myself ♪

Anyways, I didn’t think I’d bump into anyone at this hour. It’s awfully early for breakfast, isn’t it?

Sora wants to grab it as soon as the game shop opens, so Sora needs to hurry up and eat so he can leave!

I just need to spread out the plastic wrap, and mold the onigiri… and breakfast should be done in a flash ♪

Ahh~... Have you checked the forecast, Harukawa-kun?

The forecast?

A typhoon’s coming in. Apparently, strong winds and rain should be coming in a few hours. I don’t think we’re going to be allowed to go outside?

A typhoon… That’s a little worrying, but…

It should definitely be okay! Sora just needs to go pick up the game!

I get how you feel, but… It’s seriously dangerous. You shouldn’t go, okay?

Uuu… If the game store’s closed that means we can’t play any games~

Sora wanted to play with everyone in ASOBI Club…

Now, now. If you want games, there’s all kinds of other games we could play, right? Like the ones we share in ASOBI, right?

M~n. Sora knows it’s selfish of him, but… Sora is really sorry!

Haha, so that’s how it is…~

… Ah, I know. We might not be able to play the game you were planning to buy, but…

Why don’t we play a game the public hasn’t even seen yet?

Jun-chan-senpai lent us a game that hasn’t even hit the market yet! Ta-daaa…☆

Ahhh. It’s the prototype for the Idol Sugoroku game[^], huh? You become an idol and try to reach the top of the industry.

You know what it is, Yuuta-kun? Even though it’s not on the market?

Yeah. It was around summer time… Don’t you remember ASOBI club played a game called “Show Biz Sugoroku?”

Idol Sugoroku is a board game made by the same company and COSPRO was involved with helping make it.

So… See? COSPRO idols are the game pieces on the board. Look, here’s me~♪

HiHi~♪ That’s amazing~ It’s small, but they captured all your features really well!

You don’t know how happy I am to hear you say that. At first, this thing looked nothing like me.

Yup, that’s right. We love games, so me and Sazanami-senpai got to play-test it.

It took a pretty long time to get the design to look this good, y’know~

Hey now, that’s weird. I’m in COSPRO too, why wasn’t game-lovin’ Rinne-kun invited to do it~?

I don’t know the details, I just know we were the ones who were picked.

Maybe they avoided you because they knew you’d turn it into a gambling game ♪

You jerk! Lil’ ol’ me!?

It is you, so if you were part of it, you’d definitely get carried away with whatever you wanted to do.

Well, duh! I’d use my position, bada-bing, bada-boom… Gyahaha ☆

Ahaha. You’re definitely the type who shouldn’t be involved in anything, huh~

Is it really okay for us outsiders to play the prototype?

Mhm. Or rather, if anything, it’d be great if you really went for it. It’d be really helpful if you had any opinions about it.

HuHu~♪ Got it! Sora will do his best to treat it like it’s beta-testing!

Ahaha, you don’t have to go all out, you know~? At the end of the day, you’re a player too.

Anyways, let’s get things started now. The rules are basically the same as Show Biz Sugoroku, so I doubt I’ll need to explain them.

First we have to pick the pieces we’re going to play as. I’m gonna be… Well, since it’s easier to understand, I’ll choose my piece.

I’ll choose me too. I’m attached to this lil’ guy.

Then I’m gonna be me too. Time to rake in the jackpot ♪

M~n… I guess I’ll use Hinata-kun’s piece.

Then Sora will be… Umm…

Why don’t we make our own pieces so everyone feels included playing the game?

Huh? What do you mean, make them?

They’re not going to be super high quality pieces, but what do you think?

Ooh, that’s a great idea! I’d really be able to empathize with my piece if it had me on it!

While we’re at it, why don’t we draw each other and exchange them after?

Alright! Let’s go sit across each other at that table over there and draw ♪

(Thanks to the typhoon, we can’t play the game Sora wanted to play quite yet, but…)

Yeah, you’re right. There’s probably lots of things we could discover today, given we’ve got some gamers gathered together.

He~y, are you guys gonna yap forever? I’m getting tired of waiting over here~

Oops. We totally forgot about the game. Alrighty, shall we begin~?

Rinne-senpai might be a newbie, but I don’t have to hold back, do I? You’re good at this kinda game right?
