Translation Notes

  1. ↑ Shishou (師匠 lit. master) is an honorific for someone who has mastered a craft. If -shishou means "master", -daishishou (-大師匠) means "grandmaster". Sora is talking about Natsume and Wataru here.
  2. ↑ Originally, these were (in order) ちちんぷいぷい (chichinpuipui) and あぶらかたぶら (abracadabra). Chichinpuipui is a cutesy "magic spell" parents use when their kid gets hurt, used in combination with 痛いの痛いの飛んでいけ (pain, pain, go away). I localized these two so the first one becomes "abracadabra" and the second one is "alakazam".
  3. ↑ This is likely an error; Shinobu is the secretary of the student council in his third year.
