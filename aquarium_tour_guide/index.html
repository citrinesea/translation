Translation Notes

  1. ↑ Churaumi Aquarium is a tourism staple of Okinawa.
  2. ↑ Kanatee, the mascot of AOUMI Aquarium, is a blue manatee that has an ahoge like Kanata's. Manatees are not native to Japan, and are primarily imported or donated from abroad from countries like Mexico. Dugongs (an endangered species in the same order as manatees) natively live in the waters of Okinawa prefecture.
  3. ↑ Uchinaaguchi is one of the native languages of Okinawa. While Japan does not use many "ti" (てぃ) sounds like in the name "Kanatee" (かなてぃ), it is a common sound in Ryukyuan langauges. This is what Ibuki likely means.
  4. ↑ Urashima Taro is the main character of the fairy-tale the Legend of Urashimako. Urashima Taro is a fisherman who, after rescuing a sea turtle, is invited to the Palace of the Dragon God by the princess Otohime. He spends days at the palace, but gets homesick and wants to return home. Otohime gives him a box and warns him not to open it when he leaves. When he returns home, he actually realizes he's been gone for three hundred years. When he opens the box, he turns into an old man.
