Hmm, as long as you’ve reflect, there is no need for me to say any more. I shouldn’t call a woman a glutton to her face, so I shall reflect on that too.

With the apologies out of the way, I must ask— Did you get this apple from the refrigerator?

So you grabbed it from the sink, hm? If that’s the case, someone must have left it behind, so we shouldn’t just eat it as we like.

Yes, I put them in there to have after school. Exercise makes you hungry, and apple “com-po-to,” does not take long to make.
